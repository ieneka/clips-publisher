Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Clips Publisher is used only by its owner to publish videos to the owner's own TikTok accounts.

What data is used

  • The TikTok access token of the owner's accounts, stored locally on the owner's computer, to upload the videos the owner approves.
  • Basic profile information of those accounts (display name, open id) returned by TikTok when connecting them.
  • Public view counts of the owner's own published videos, for the owner's analytics.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect data from other TikTok users.
  • We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.
  • We do not use data for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on the owner's computer and is deleted when an account is disconnected. To revoke access, remove the app in TikTok: Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services permissions.
