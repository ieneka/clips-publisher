Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Clips Publisher is a desktop tool used only by its owner to publish videos to the owner's own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts. It runs on the owner's computer and is not offered to other people.

YouTube API Services

Clips Publisher uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

  • What we access: only the permission to upload videos to the owner's own channel (OAuth scope youtube.upload). We do not access, read or store any other YouTube data: no channel lists, comments, subscribers, analytics or data about other users.
  • How we use it: to upload the clips the owner reviewed, with the title and description the owner approved, and to schedule their publication time.
  • Storage: the OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only in a local file on the owner's computer. They are never sent to any server other than Google's, and never shared, sold or used for advertising.
  • Revoking access: access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page, https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions (remove "Clips Publisher"). The tool then stops uploading.
  • Deletion: the stored tokens are deleted when the account is disconnected, and in any case within 7 days of a deletion request to the contact below or of access being revoked.

TikTok

The TikTok access token of the owner's accounts is stored locally to upload the videos the owner approves, together with the basic profile information TikTok returns when connecting (display name, open id). To revoke access, remove the app in TikTok: Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services permissions.

Instagram

An Instagram access token of the owner's professional account is stored locally to publish the Reels the owner approves and to read their view counts. To revoke access, remove the app in Instagram: Settings, Website permissions, Apps and websites.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect data from other users of TikTok, YouTube or Instagram.
  • We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.
  • We do not use data for advertising, and we do not use cookies or trackers on these pages.

Retention and deletion

All data stays on the owner's computer. Tokens are deleted when an account is disconnected or within 7 days of a request. Public view counts of the owner's own videos are kept for the owner's analytics until the owner deletes them.
