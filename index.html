Clips Publisher

Clips Publisher is a personal desktop tool used by its owner to prepare short vertical clips from footage the owner is licensed to use (official material provided by content campaigns) and to publish them to the owner's own accounts.

  • TikTok (Content Posting API)
  • YouTube Shorts (YouTube API Services)
  • Instagram Reels (Instagram API)

Every clip and caption is reviewed by the owner before it is posted. The tool never posts without the owner's explicit confirmation and never posts to accounts the owner does not own.

YouTube

Clips Publisher uses YouTube API Services to upload the owner's clips as Shorts to the owner's own YouTube channel (scope youtube.upload only). It does not read, store or display data from other YouTube users or channels. By using Clips Publisher you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies.
