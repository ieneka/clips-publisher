Clips Publisher

Clips Publisher is a personal desktop tool used by its owner to prepare short vertical clips from footage the owner is licensed to use (official material provided by content campaigns) and to publish them to the owner's own TikTok accounts.

Every clip and caption is reviewed on the owner's phone before it is posted. The tool never posts without the owner's explicit confirmation and never posts to accounts the owner does not own.
